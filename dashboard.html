How the bot learns

Record first; adjust only with evidence

Every outcome adds one observation. Patterns are compared by trend alignment, entry price, evidence score, volume, and momentum. No trade result changes rules automatically.

Safety rule: one trade is not proof of cause. Threshold updates remain manual, require larger cohorts, regression tests, and out-of-sample paper validation.

Evidence score

A rule-based evidence score from 0 to 100. It is not a percent chance of winning.

Bot win probability

Not calculated until the strategy has a calibrated model that has been tested against enough historical outcomes.

Market-implied estimate

A rough price-based indication from the contract ask. It includes market pricing and can differ from the true outcome probability.

Historical win rate

The proportion of all settled journal trades recorded as wins. It is not a probability forecast for one particular signal.
