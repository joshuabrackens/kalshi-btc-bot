Learn the terms

Plain-English definitions for the numbers on this page.

TradingView alert

A message from your chart indicator. It tells the bot what the indicator noticed, but it does not automatically mean a trade is made.

UP / DOWN

The alert’s suggested direction. It describes which outcome the signal points toward; it is not a guarantee.

Timeframe

How much time each candle on the indicator chart represents. A 1-minute chart and a 15-minute contract are different things.

Strike price

The reference BTC price used to determine the market’s final UP/DOWN outcome according to the market’s rules.

Evidence score

A score from the bot’s programmed rules. A score of 60/100 does not mean a 60% chance to win.

Ask price

The displayed price to buy one contract, usually shown in cents. Fees may make the total cost higher.

Win rate

The share of completed trades that were winners. It does not show how large the wins or losses were, so read it with net profit/loss.

Net profit / loss

The combined result of settled practice trades after the fees recorded by the bot. It is only as accurate as the trade and fee records.
